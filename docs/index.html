Built for privacy.
Designed for simplicity.

Everything you need for voice recording and journaling, nothing you don't.

One-Tap Recording

Start recording instantly. Pause, resume, or save with a single tap. Crash recovery ensures you never lose your recordings.

On-Device Transcription

Powered by Whisper AI. Download English models and transcribe offline. Your audio never leaves your device.

Organized Library

Search, label, and favorite your recordings. 30-day recently deleted folder keeps your work safe.

Encrypted by Default

Database encrypted with SQLCipher. Keys stored in Keychain. No analytics, no tracking, no cloud sync.

Universal App

Works beautifully on iPhone, iPad, and Android. Responsive layouts adapt to any screen size, portrait or landscape.

Daily Prompts

Gentle reminders and thought-provoking prompts. Set a daily recording goal and track your progress.

Privacy by design,
not by accident.

Your recordings live in your device's private storage. The database is encrypted at rest. Transcription happens on-device with downloadable AI models. No servers, no accounts, no backdoors.

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No automatic cloud sync
  • Audio stays on your device
  • Open source for transparency
